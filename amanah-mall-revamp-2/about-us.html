Our story

Where shoppingbecomes a day out

Amanah Mall is Lahore's largest and most-visited retail mall, set right in the heart of Model Town Link Road.

More than 500,000 people come through our doors every month. The building rises eight floors above four basement levels of parking, so a quick errand easily turns into a whole afternoon.

The lower four floors are given over to shopping. Above them sit the food court, Magic Planet, Velocity Fitness Club and Cinepax Cinema. More recent additions include a trampoline park, an indoor amusement park, a VR park and a four-screen cinema.

Who we are

The AmanahGroup

Amanah Mall is part of the Amanah Group, an international business group built on innovation and excellence.

The group has worked in real estate since 1995, with a track record in commercial building development across the United Kingdom, the UAE and Pakistan.

Its approach is simple: build relationships that last, construct to a high standard, look after customers, and deliver on what it promises.
