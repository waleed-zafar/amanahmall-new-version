Entertainment & experiences

Play is onevery floor

Bounce, bowl, game and watch the latest blockbusters. Play areas are open Mon–Sat 12 PM – 9 PM and Sundays 2 PM – 9 PM.

  • 01

    Magic Planet

    Indoor amusement park

    Kids' amusements and the VR Park — the mall's family destination for rides and games.

  • 02

    Trampoline Park

    Bounce & play

    High-energy jumping zones for kids, teens and families — one of the biggest draws at Amanah Mall.

  • 03

    Bowling & Snooker

    At Magic Planet

    Lanes for friendly rivalries plus the American Pool & Snooker Club, both part of Magic Planet.

  • 04

    Fun Factory Playland

    Kids' play zone

    A safe, colourful play space built for little ones to explore while families shop and dine.

  • 05

    Cinepax Cinema

    Latest releases · 3D

    Catch the newest films on big screens, including 3D — the perfect end to a day at the mall.

  • 06

    Velocity Fitness Club

    Gym · Mon–Sat 7 AM – 10 PM

    A full fitness club inside the mall, open early so you can train before the shops open.
