  • Pakistan's largest indoor trampoline facility. It has interconnected trampolines, foam pits, trapezes, slam-dunk basketball zones, dodgeball courts and more, on the mall's entertainment floors.

  • It's on the 2nd floor of Amanah Mall. The play areas are open Monday to Saturday, 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, and Sunday, 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

  • Foam pit jumps, slam-dunk trampolines, dodgeball courts, wall-run zones, trapeze and freestyle jump areas, and designated kids' bouncing zones for younger children.
